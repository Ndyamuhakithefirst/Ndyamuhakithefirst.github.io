Technologies

Our Capabilities

Multiple print technologies under one roof — we match the process to your requirements.

FDM Printing

Workhorse of Rapid Manufacturing

The most versatile and cost-effective 3D printing process. Excellent for structural prototypes, jigs, fixtures, and end-use functional parts. We run calibrated FDM machines 24/7 for the fastest possible lead times.

Build Volume
400 × 400 × 450mm
Layer Height
0.05mm – 0.3mm
Tolerance
± 0.2mm
Lead Time
24 – 72 hours
Available Materials
PLA+ ABS PETG TPU / Flex ASA Nylon PA6 PC Blend Carbon Fibre PLA

SLA Resin

Microscopic Detail, Flawless Surfaces

Photopolymer resin cured by UV laser — the process of choice when you need surface quality and fine features that FDM cannot match. Ideal for visual prototypes, dental, jewellery, and display models.

Build Volume
218 × 123 × 260mm
Layer Height
0.025mm – 0.1mm
Tolerance
± 0.1mm
Lead Time
24 – 48 hours
Available Materials
Standard Resin ABS-Like Resin Castable Wax Flexible Resin High-Temp Resin Clear Resin

SLS / Nylon

Production-Grade Without Supports

Selective Laser Sintering fuses nylon powder without support structures — perfect for complex geometries, interlocking assemblies, and short production runs. Parts are isotropic and ready for functional testing.

Build Volume
165 × 165 × 300mm
Layer Height
0.1mm
Tolerance
± 0.3mm
Lead Time
3 – 5 business days
Available Materials
PA12 Nylon PA11 Nylon Glass-Filled Nylon TPU Powder
